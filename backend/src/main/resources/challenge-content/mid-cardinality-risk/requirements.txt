cardinality(tagSizes) returns the product of the tag cardinalities; an empty list is a single series, so its cardinality is 1.
A tag size of zero makes the product zero; null tags or negative sizes throw IllegalArgumentException.
Guard against overflow: clamp the product at Long.MAX_VALUE instead of wrapping around.
risky(tagSizes) returns true when the cardinality exceeds the documented limit of 10000 active series.
