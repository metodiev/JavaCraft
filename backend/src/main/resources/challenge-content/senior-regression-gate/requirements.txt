Report the signed percentage change as "<verdict> <change>", where change is formatted with Locale.ROOT to one decimal place, for example FAIL +12.5%.
The change ratio is (candidateP99 - baselineP99) / baselineP99.
PASS when the change ratio is at most tolerance, WARN when it is at most twice the tolerance, and FAIL beyond that.
baselineP99 must be positive, candidateP99 must not be negative, and tolerance must be finite and not negative; otherwise throw IllegalArgumentException.
