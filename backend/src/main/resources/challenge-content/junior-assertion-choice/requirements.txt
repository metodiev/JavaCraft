Choose the assertion method that expresses a requested check and return its AssertJ method name.
"equality" maps to "isEqualTo" and "null" maps to "isNull".
"not-null" maps to "isNotNull" and "exception" maps to "isInstanceOf".
"collection-size" maps to "hasSize" and "collection-empty" maps to "isEmpty".
Any other check, including null, maps to "unknown".
Check names are matched ignoring case.
