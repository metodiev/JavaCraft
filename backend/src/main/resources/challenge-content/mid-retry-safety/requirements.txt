GET, HEAD, PUT, OPTIONS and TRACE are safe methods; POST, PATCH and DELETE are unsafe.
A request is retryable when the status is one of 429, 500, 502, 503 and 504 and either the method is safe or an idempotency key is present.
Success and other client error statuses are never retried.
Match the method case-insensitively after trimming.
Throw IllegalArgumentException for a null or unknown method and for a status below 100 or above 599.
