codeNames maps each concept to the term used in the code, and glossary maps the same concept to the agreed ubiquitous-language term.
Two terms match when they are equal after trimming surrounding spaces and ignoring case, so 'Order Total' and ' order total ' match.
Report "concept: code uses 'X' but glossary says 'Y'" for every concept whose terms do not match, showing the trimmed names with their original case.
Concepts missing from the glossary and entries with a null concept key are ignored.
A null name is treated as an empty string.
