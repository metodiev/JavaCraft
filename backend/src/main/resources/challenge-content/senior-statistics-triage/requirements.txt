findings inspects a map of Hibernate statistics names to values and returns one message per suspicious metric, sorted alphabetically, or an empty list for a null or empty map.
"slowest query <n>ms" is reported when queryExecutionMaxTime exceeds 1000 milliseconds.
"statements not reused" is reported when prepareStatementCount is at least 4 times queryExecutionCount, which indicates JDBC statement caching is off.
"collections eagerly fetched" is reported when collectionFetchCount is at least 90 percent of entityLoadCount, meaning most loads come from eager collections that should be lazy.
A metric that is absent from the map is ignored, and a ratio test is skipped when its denominator is zero or missing.
