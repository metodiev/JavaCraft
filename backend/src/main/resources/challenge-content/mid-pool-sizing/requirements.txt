Size the pool with ceil(databaseCores * (1 + waitBudgetMillis / serviceTimeMillis)), since more waiting allows more concurrent connections.
Never return less than 2 connections, so the service can still overlap work.
Cap the pool at eight times databaseCores, because more connections than that only move the queue into the database.
databaseCores must be at least 1, serviceTimeMillis must be positive and finite, and waitBudgetMillis must be finite and not negative; otherwise throw IllegalArgumentException.
