Keep a share of the error traces: ceil(errors * errorSampleRate), so an errorSampleRate of 1.0 keeps every error.
Keep a baseline sample of the non-error traces: ceil((traces - errors) * baselineRate).
The result is the two samples added together, never more than traces.
traces must not be negative, errors must be within 0..traces, and both rates must be finite and within 0..1; otherwise throw IllegalArgumentException.
