A method is worth extracting when it mixes two or more distinct responsibilities, when it is longer than twenty lines, or when it uses eight or more local variables.
A method of six lines or fewer is never extracted, even when the responsibility count, the length or the variable count signal extraction.
When the method has seven or more lines, any one of the three signals is enough on its own: two or more responsibilities, more than twenty lines, or eight or more used variables.
Otherwise a single responsibility, twenty lines or fewer and seven used variables or fewer leave the method in place.
