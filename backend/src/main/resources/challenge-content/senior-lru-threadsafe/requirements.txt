Implement a thread-safe fixed-capacity least-recently-used cache with get, put and size.
get and put are atomic with respect to each other, and size never exceeds the capacity, even under concurrent access.
get returns the cached value or null for a missing key, and marks the entry as most recently used; put inserts or updates and makes the entry most recently used.
Evict the least recently used entry when a put would exceed the capacity.
Constructing with a non-positive capacity throws IllegalArgumentException.
