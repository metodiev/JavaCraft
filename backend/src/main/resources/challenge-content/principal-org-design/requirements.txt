Report findings in the order ownership, service count, platform team.
When clearOwnership is false report "ownership is unclear: assign one accountable team per service".
When servicesPerTeam is below 1 report "<n> services per team is invalid: a team must own at least one service"; when it is above 10 report "<n> services per team is too many: split the team or merge the services".
When platformTeamExists is false report "no platform team: create one to own shared build, deploy and observability tooling".
A topology with no findings returns exactly "topology is healthy: ownership is clear and services are sized well".
