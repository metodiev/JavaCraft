Judge a test name by whether it states a condition and an expected outcome.
A condition word is one of: when, given, if.
An expectation word is one of: then, should, returns, throws.
Report "missing-condition" when no condition word appears and "missing-expectation" when no expectation word appears, in that order.
Names are split into words at non-letter characters and camel-case boundaries and matched case-insensitively.
A null or blank name reports both problems, and a name containing both parts reports none.
