State flows between CLOSED, OPEN and HALF_OPEN; unknown or null states are rejected with IllegalArgumentException.
CLOSED keeps normal traffic and moves to OPEN when a call fails and failureRate has reached threshold.
OPEN ignores call outcomes and stays OPEN until a recovery probe begins.
HALF_OPEN closes on a successful probe and reopens on a failed probe.
failureRate must be 0..100 and threshold must be 1..100; both are validated.
