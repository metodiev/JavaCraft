Triage starts from the user-visible symptom, so the first step is always confirm-user-impact, never the loudest alert.
When recentChange is true, check-recent-changes comes second because most incidents follow a deploy or configuration change.
Then add the active signals in this fixed order: inspect-errors, inspect-latency, inspect-saturation.
When no signal is active, add widen-scope before the final step; the last step is always form-hypothesis.
