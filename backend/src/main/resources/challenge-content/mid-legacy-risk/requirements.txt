Classify risk as HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW from complexity, testCoveragePercent and hasRecentIncidents.
Complexity above 20 is high risk, complexity above 10 up to 20 is medium risk, and complexity of 10 or less is low risk.
Test coverage below 50 percent raises the risk one level and coverage below 25 percent raises it two levels.
Any recent incident makes the risk high.
Coverage and incidents only ever raise the risk, never lower it, so complexity 3 with 20 percent coverage is HIGH while complexity 3 with 50 percent coverage is LOW.
