Apply the documented static analysis quality gate rules to a list of severities.
A "critical" or "vulnerability" finding anywhere in the list fails the gate with "FAIL".
Blockers fail with "FAIL" only when their count is greater than blockerThreshold, otherwise they warn.
"minor" and "info" findings warn, and an empty list passes with "PASS".
An unrecognised severity, a null list or a null entry is malformed and warns with "WARN" unless a failing rule above already applies.
Severities are matched ignoring case and surrounding whitespace.
